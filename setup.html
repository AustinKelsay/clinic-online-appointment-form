🚀 Let's Set Up Your Appointment System!

What We're Building

We're creating an online appointment booking system that:

  • Shows available appointment times on your website
  • Lets patients book appointments online
  • Saves all appointments to a Google Spreadsheet
  • Can be managed entirely through Google Sheets (no coding needed!)

Time needed: About 30-45 minutes for first-time setup

📊 Part 1: Create Your Google Spreadsheet

1Create a New Spreadsheet

  1. Open your web browser and go to sheets.google.com
  2. Sign in with your Google account (or create one if needed)
  3. Click the big + Blank button to create a new spreadsheet
  4. At the top left where it says "Untitled spreadsheet", click and rename it to: CoolClinic System
Tip: Keep this spreadsheet open in a separate tab - you'll need it throughout the setup!

2Save Your Spreadsheet ID

Look at your browser's address bar. You'll see a long URL like this:

https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ABC123def456GHI789jkl/edit#gid=0

The highlighted part is your Spreadsheet ID. You'll need this later!

Important: Copy this ID somewhere safe (like a notepad). You'll need it in Part 3!

3Set Up the Appointments Sheet

Your spreadsheet already has one sheet (look at the bottom - it says "Sheet1"). Let's set it up:

  1. Right-click on "Sheet1" at the bottom
  2. Click "Rename" and change it to: Appointments
  3. Click on cell A1 and paste these headers:
Timestamp Name Phone Appointment Date Preferred Time Slots Reason for Visit Status
After pasting, press Tab to move across cells. The headers should spread across columns A through G.

4Create the Available Slots Sheet

  1. At the bottom of your spreadsheet, click the + button to add a new sheet
  2. Right-click the new sheet and rename it to: Available_Slots
  3. Click on cell A1 of this new sheet
  4. Copy and paste this entire table:
Date 9:00 AM - 10:00 AM 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM 2:00 PM - 3:00 PM 3:00 PM - 4:00 PM 4:00 PM - 5:00 PM 2024-01-15 AVAILABLE AVAILABLE BOOKED AVAILABLE AVAILABLE BLOCKED 2024-01-16 AVAILABLE BOOKED AVAILABLE AVAILABLE BOOKED AVAILABLE 2024-01-17 BLOCKED AVAILABLE AVAILABLE AVAILABLE AVAILABLE AVAILABLE 2024-01-18 AVAILABLE AVAILABLE AVAILABLE BLOCKED AVAILABLE AVAILABLE 2024-01-19 AVAILABLE AVAILABLE BOOKED AVAILABLE AVAILABLE BLOCKED 2024-01-22 AVAILABLE AVAILABLE AVAILABLE AVAILABLE AVAILABLE AVAILABLE
Note: You'll need to update these dates to current/future dates before going live!

🔑 Part 2: Get Your Google API Credentials

This part might seem technical, but just follow each step carefully. We're getting special permission for your website to access your Google Sheets.

1Go to Google Cloud Console

  1. Open a new tab and go to console.cloud.google.com
  2. Sign in with the same Google account you used for Sheets
  3. You might see a welcome screen - just click "Select a project" at the top

2Create a New Project

  1. Click "NEW PROJECT" (usually in the top right)
  2. For Project name, enter: CoolClinic Scheduler
  3. Leave Location as "No organization"
  4. Click "CREATE"
  5. Wait about 30 seconds for it to create
Google will show a notification when your project is ready. Make sure you're working in your new project (check the dropdown at the top of the page).

3Enable Google Sheets API

  1. In the search bar at the top, type "Google Sheets API" and press Enter
  2. Click on "Google Sheets API" from the results
  3. Click the big blue "ENABLE" button
  4. Wait for it to enable (about 10 seconds)

4Create Service Account

  1. Click the menu (☰) in the top left
  2. Find and click "APIs & Services" → "Credentials"
  3. Click "+ CREATE CREDENTIALS" at the top
  4. Choose "Service account"
  5. Fill in:
    • Service account name: coolclinic-bot
    • Service account ID: (it fills automatically)
  6. Click "CREATE AND CONTINUE"
  7. Skip the optional steps by clicking "CONTINUE" then "DONE"

5Download Your Secret Key

  1. You should now see your service accounts list
  2. Click on "coolclinic-bot@..." (the email address)
  3. Click the "KEYS" tab at the top
  4. Click "ADD KEY" → "Create new key"
  5. Choose "JSON" format
  6. Click "CREATE"
  7. A file will download - save it somewhere safe!
Critical: This file is like a password. Keep it safe and never share it publicly!

6Get the Service Account Email

  1. Open the JSON file you just downloaded (use Notepad or any text editor)
  2. Look for a line that says "client_email"
  3. Copy the email address (looks like: coolclinic-bot@coolclinic-scheduler-123456.iam.gserviceaccount.com)
  4. Keep this file open - you'll need the whole contents soon!

7Share Your Spreadsheet

  1. Go back to your "CoolClinic System" Google Sheet
  2. Click the "Share" button (top right)
  3. Paste the service account email you just copied
  4. Make sure it's set to "Editor" (not Viewer)
  5. Uncheck "Notify people"
  6. Click "Share"
You're giving your website's "robot assistant" permission to read and write to your spreadsheet!

🔌 Part 3: Connect Everything in Replit

1Open Replit Secrets

  1. In your Replit project, look at the left sidebar
  2. Click the "Secrets" icon (looks like a lock 🔒)
  3. You'll see a place to add secrets

2Add Your Google Credentials

  1. Click "New Secret"
  2. For Key, type exactly: GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_KEY
  3. For Value:
    • Go back to your downloaded JSON file
    • Select ALL the text (Ctrl+A or Cmd+A)
    • Copy it (Ctrl+C or Cmd+C)
    • Paste it into the Value box
  4. Click "Add Secret"
Make sure you copy the ENTIRE file contents, including the { } brackets!

3Add Your Spreadsheet ID

  1. Click "New Secret" again
  2. For Key, type exactly: GOOGLE_SHEET_ID
  3. For Value, paste the Spreadsheet ID you saved from Part 1, Step 2
  4. Click "Add Secret"

4Test Everything!

  1. Click the "Run" button at the top of Replit
  2. Wait for the app to start
  3. Open your website in a new tab
  4. Fill out the appointment form and submit
  5. Go back to your Google Sheet
  6. Check the "Appointments" sheet - you should see your test appointment!
If you see your appointment in the spreadsheet, congratulations! Everything is working!

🛠️ Part 4: Daily Management (For Nurses/Staff)

Once everything is set up, managing appointments is easy! Here's what your staff needs to know:

📅 Daily Tasks (5 minutes)

  1. Check New Appointments:
    • Open the Google Sheet
    • Go to "Appointments" sheet
    • Look for new entries (they appear at the bottom)
    • Call patients to confirm their appointments
    • Add "CONFIRMED" in the Status column (column G)
  2. Update Tomorrow's Availability:
    • Go to "Available_Slots" sheet
    • Find tomorrow's date
    • Update any slots that need to be blocked

📋 Weekly Tasks (20 minutes)

  1. Add Next Week's Dates:
    • Go to "Available_Slots" sheet
    • Add 5-7 new rows at the bottom
    • Enter next week's dates (format: YYYY-MM-DD)
    • Set all slots to "AVAILABLE" initially
    • Block any known unavailable times
  2. Archive Old Appointments:
    • In "Appointments" sheet, sort by date
    • Copy appointments older than 1 month
    • Paste them in a separate "Archive" spreadsheet
    • Delete the old rows to keep sheet fast
  3. Review Patterns:
    • Look for popular time slots
    • Consider adding more availability during busy times

🕐 Managing Time Slots

Slot Status Options:

  • AVAILABLE - Patients can book this slot
  • BOOKED - Already taken by a patient
  • BLOCKED - Not available (lunch, meetings, etc.)

Common Scenarios:

  • Doctor calls in sick: Change all their slots to "BLOCKED"
  • Patient cancels: Change slot back to "AVAILABLE"
  • Double booking: Call patients to reschedule, update sheet
  • Add extra hours: Add new time columns or extend dates

Pro Tips:

  • Use conditional formatting to color-code statuses
  • Add notes in column H for special cases
  • Keep a "Waiting List" sheet for popular times

❓ Troubleshooting Common Issues

"Spreadsheet not found" Error

The website can't access your Google Sheet.

Solution: Make sure you shared the spreadsheet with the service account email (the long one ending in .iam.gserviceaccount.com). It must have "Editor" permissions.
Appointments Not Showing Up

Form submits but nothing appears in the sheet.

Solution: Check that both secret keys in Replit are correct. The JSON key should include ALL the text from the file, including brackets.
Can't Find Service Account Email

Don't know which email to share the sheet with.

Solution: Open your downloaded JSON file in a text editor. Look for "client_email" - the email is right after it.
Google Cloud Console is Confusing

Can't find the right buttons or options.

Solution: Make sure you're in the right project (check the dropdown at the top). Use the search bar to find "Google Sheets API" and "Credentials" directly.

✅ Final Checklist

Before going live, make sure you've:

  • Created both sheets: "Appointments" and "Available_Slots"
  • Updated the dates in Available_Slots to current/future dates
  • Shared the spreadsheet with your service account
  • Added both secrets to Replit (GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_KEY and GOOGLE_SHEET_ID)
  • Tested by submitting an appointment
  • Trained staff on daily management
  • Bookmarked the Google Sheet for easy access
You're ready to go! Your online appointment system is now live. Patients can book appointments 24/7, and your staff can manage everything through Google Sheets!
