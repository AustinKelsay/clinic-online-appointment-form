# CoolClinic - Online Appointment System 🏥

Welcome to your online appointment booking system! This app lets patients book appointments through your website, and all appointments are automatically saved to a Google Spreadsheet that your staff can manage.

## 🚀 Quick Start (How to Run Your App)

### Starting Your App:
1. Look for the big green **"Run"** button at the top of your Replit screen
2. Click it and wait about 10-15 seconds
3. A small browser window will appear on the right showing your website
4. Click "Open in a new tab" to see your full website

That's it! Your appointment system is now running.

### Stopping Your App:
- Click the **"Stop"** button (appears where the Run button was)
- The app will stop, but all your data is safely saved

## 📱 Your App Has Three Pages

1. **Home Page** (`index.html`) - Where patients book appointments
2. **Setup Guide** (`setup.html`) - Step-by-step instructions for connecting Google Sheets
3. **Admin Page** - Coming soon!

### To Access the Setup Guide:
After running your app, add `/setup.html` to the end of your website address.

Example:
- Your app URL: `https://coolclinic.yourname.repl.co`
- Setup page: `https://coolclinic.yourname.repl.co/setup.html`

## 🗂️ Understanding Your Replit Workspace

Think of Replit like a virtual computer in the cloud. Here's what you see:

### Left Sidebar - Your Files:
- **app.py** - The brain of your app (handles form submissions)
- **index.html** - Your main appointment booking page
- **setup.html** - Setup instructions page
- **style.css** - Makes everything look pretty
- **script.js** - Makes the forms interactive

### Middle Area - Code Editor:
- This is where you can view and edit files
- Don't worry about editing code - everything is already set up!

### Right Side - Preview Window:
- Shows your running website
- Updates automatically when you make changes

### Important Buttons:
- **🔒 Secrets** - Where you'll add your Google credentials (see setup guide)
- **📦 Packages** - Shows installed tools (already configured)
- **🔄 Version Control** - Saves backups of your work

## 🔑 First-Time Setup Required

Before patients can book appointments, you need to connect Google Sheets:

1. Run your app (click the Run button)
2. Open the setup page (add `/setup.html` to your URL)
3. Follow the step-by-step guide there
4. This takes about 30-45 minutes the first time

## 💡 How It Works

```
Patient fills form → Submits appointment → Saves to Google Sheets → Staff reviews & confirms
```

Once set up:
- Patients can book appointments 24/7
- All appointments appear in your Google Spreadsheet
- Staff manages everything through familiar Google Sheets
- No technical knowledge needed for daily use!

## 🆘 Getting Help

### If the app won't start:
1. Try clicking "Stop" then "Run" again
2. Refresh your browser (F5 or Cmd+R)
3. Check that all files are present in the sidebar

### If forms aren't working:
1. Make sure you've completed the Google Sheets setup
2. Check that your Secrets are added (🔒 icon)
3. Look for error messages in the Console (bottom of screen)

### Common Questions:

**Q: Is my data safe?**  
A: Yes! All appointments are stored in your private Google Sheets. Only people you give access can see them.

**Q: Can multiple staff members access the appointments?**  
A: Yes! Just share the Google Spreadsheet with their email addresses.

**Q: What if I close Replit?**  
A: No problem! Your app stays running for a while, and all data is saved in Google Sheets.

**Q: How do patients access the booking page?**  
A: Share your Replit app URL with them. You can add this link to your main website or social media.
